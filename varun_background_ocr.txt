PERSONAL RECORDS AND MEMOIRS OF C-402 EPSILON VARUN

In Service to the Omnissiah: The Chronicles of C-402 Epsilon Varun

I am C-402 Epsilon Varun, Magos Biologis and Explorator of the Adeptus Mecehanicus,
Veteran Artifex of the Ordo Reductor, Master of Medicae Arts and Xenobiology, Inquisitor's
Ally, Data-Savant, and Custodian of the Armorium. This is my chronice, a testament to my journey from the forges of Mars to the shadowed halls of the Inquisition.

The Ascension of the Tech-Priest
My initiation into the mysteries of the Omnissiah was rigorous, a path fraught with peril and enlightment.
My first distinction came upon the forsaken world of Thalasson V, a planet shrouded in perpetual storms and ancient secrets. 
It was here that I led an expedition into the depths of a forgotten Mechanicus outpost,lost to the annals of history.
Our goal: to recover and STC fragment rumored to hold the secrets of advanced biogenetic augmentation.

Descending into the abyssal depths, we faced relentless electro-storms and hostile xenos
fauna. My cogitators whirred and clicked, Processing data at unfathomable speeds as I
navigated the treacherous corridors. It was there, in the heart of the complex, that I
encountered the ancient guardian—a colossal construct of steel and wrath. Engaging in a
battle of wits and ferocity, I outmaneuvered the guardian, disabling it with a precise EMP
pulse.

The STC fragment was secured, but not without cost. I sustained grievous injuries, and it
was my mastery of the Medicae Arts that allowed me to stabilize my own condition,
performing a self-surgery that would later be chronicled in the annals of the Mechanicus,
This feat earned me the title of Artifex of the Ordo Reductor, a rare honor bestowed upon
those who have achieved unparalleled feats of engineering and combat.

The Inquisitor's Call

My second defining moment came under the auspices of Inquisitor Alaric Voss. Tasked
with investigating the heretical cult of the Bleeding Cog, I was to infiltrate their ranks and
dismantle their operations from within. It was a mission fraught with danger, for the cult
was adept in the dark arts of forbidden technology. By my side was Lucas Rio, an
interrogator of exceptional skill, whose silver tongue could turn the tide of any negotiation.

Together, we delved into the underbelly of Hive Secundus. Lucas, with his effortless charm,
infiltrated the cult's inner circle, gaining their trust while I posed as a rogue tech-priest
seeking forbidden knowledge. The ruse was intricate, a dance of deception and subtlety.
Lucas's words wove a tapestry of lies so convincing that even the most paranoid cultists
were beguiled.

As we neared the cult's sanctum, our true purpose became clear. We were to disable their
warp-tainted generator, a device capable of summoning daemonic entities. The ritual was
underway, and time was against us. In the ensuing chaos, Lucas's negotiations bought us
precious seconds as I hacked into the generator's core. My mechadendrites worked with
mechanical precision, severing conduits and rerouting power, culminating in a controlled
detonation that eradicated the cult and their vile machine.

This mission solidified my place in the Inquisitor's retinue. My adeptness at blending my
quest for knowledge with unwavering loyalty to the Imperium marked me as an invaluable
asset. The title of Data-Savant was bestowed upon me, acknowledging my insatiable thirst
for knowledge, both sacred and forbidden.

The Eternal Quest

Now, as the Imperium faces its darkest hour in millennia, my purpose is clear. Each
mission is a crucible, testing my resolve and ingenuity. The whispers of forbidden
knowledge continue to tempt me, but my duty to the Omnissiah and the Imperium remains
paramount. Every artifact recovered, every heretic purged, brings us one step closer to
salvation.

In the name of the Omnissiah and the Emperor, my journey continues.
